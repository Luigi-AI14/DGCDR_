You will be given an explanation for a recommended item and the review the user wrote about the recommended item.

Your task is to rate the explanation on one metric.

Please make sure you read and understand these instructions carefully.

Evaluation Criteria:
Sentiment Coherence (1-5):
- 1 (Polarity Contradiction): Direct sentiment clash. The explanation presents as a positive selling point an aspect that the user explicitly criticized, disliked, or warned against (or vice versa).
- 2 (Poor Sentiment Alignment): Noticeable sentiment dissonance. The explanation fails to recognize the user's significant reservations or exaggerates enthusiasm where the user felt indifference or mild dissatisfaction.
- 3 (Generic / Neutral Coherence): The explanation maintains a generic positive promotional tone that neither contradicts nor accurately captures the nuanced emotional reactions of the user.
- 4 (Good Sentiment Coherence): The explanation's tone and appreciation of specific features align well with the user's sentiment, with only minor discrepancies in emphasis or nuance.
- 5 (Flawless Sentiment Coherence): Complete affective congruence. The explanation perfectly mirrors the exact sentiment, intensity, and nuanced attitude (enthusiasm, caution, trade-off appreciation) expressed by the user for every shared aspect.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Identify Key Sentiments: Extract the primary sentiments expressed by the user in their review, including both positive and negative comments.
2. Compare Sentiments: Compare the user's sentiments with the explanation provided, looking for alignment, discrepancies, or contradictions.
3. Assess Tone and Emphasis: Evaluate whether the explanation's tone and emphasis on specific features match the user's sentiment, intensity, and nuanced attitude.
4. Score Sentiment Coherence: Based on the comparison, score the explanation's Sentiment Coherence on a scale of 1-5, considering the degree of alignment, dissonance, or contradiction between the explanation and the user's review.

Output Constraint:
Follow the evaluation form strictly. Output ONLY the score integer (1-5). Do not write any explanations, justifications, labels, or additional text.
