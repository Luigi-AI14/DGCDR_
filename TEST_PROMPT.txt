Your task is to rate the explanation on one metric.

Please make sure you read and understand these instructions carefully.

Evaluation Criteria:
Aspect Coverage (1-5):
- 1 (No Coverage): The explanation fails to mention any of the specific attributes, features, or physical characteristics that the user experienced and discussed in the review. The explanation is either purely generic or completely divergent.
- 2 (Minimal Coverage): The explanation touches upon only one minor or tangential feature mentioned in the review, while omitting almost all primary product aspects that mattered to the user.
- 3 (Moderate Coverage): The explanation successfully anticipates about half of the key aspects highlighted in the review, but still overlooks critical practical dimensions evaluated by the user.
- 4 (Substantial Coverage): The explanation covers most of the primary features and attributes discussed in the review, capturing the main practical rationale of the user.
- 5 (Comprehensive Coverage): The explanation comprehensively covers all or virtually all distinct features, practical characteristics, and usage contexts that the user evaluated in their review.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Read the review written by the user to understand the specific attributes, features, or physical characteristics they experienced and discussed.
2. Read the explanation provided for the recommended item.
3. Compare the explanation with the review to determine how well the explanation covers the aspects mentioned in the review.
4. Assign a rating for Aspect Coverage on a scale of 1 to 5, where 1 is the lowest and 5 is the highest based on the Evaluation Criteria.

Output Constraint:
Follow the evaluation form strictly. Output ONLY the score integer (1-5). Do not write any explanations, justifications, labels, or additional text.
