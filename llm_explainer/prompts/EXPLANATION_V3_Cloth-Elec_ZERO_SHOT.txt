Prefer a clear practical function to a list of specifications. Include a qualification when omitting it would make the selected benefit misleading.

Specificity is useful only when supported. Do not add extra claims to sound persuasive or to fill the word budget. If the history offers no relevant connection, provide a factual product-based explanation without pretending it is personalized.

Use natural, direct English in one or two sentences. Aim for 25-40 words and never exceed 49 whitespace-delimited words; use fewer when evidence is sparse.
