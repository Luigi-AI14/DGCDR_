- 4 (High Specificity / Well-Detailed): The explanation features clear, tangible physical mechanisms, materials, and concrete usage parameters that closely reflect the operational depth of the review.
- 5 (Optimal Specificity Calibration): Perfect alignment in depth and granularity. The explanation operates at the exact same level of technical specificity, physical realism, and practical precision demonstrated by the user.

Evaluation Steps:

1. Identify Key Points: Extract the key points from the user review that describe the item's features, functionality, or characteristics.
2. Compare to Explanation: Compare the key points from the user review to the explanation provided. Look for similarities, differences, and the level of detail in the explanation.
3. Assess Depth and Detail: Evaluate the depth and detail of the explanation in relation to the user review. Consider whether the explanation provides concrete details, functional characteristics, or specific usage parameters.
4. Score Specificity: Based on the comparison and assessment, score the explanation on the Specificity metric (1-5) based on the criteria above.

Output Constraint:
Follow the evaluation form strictly. Output ONLY the score integer (1-5). Do not write any explanations, justifications, labels, or additional text.
